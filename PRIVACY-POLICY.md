## 1. Overview
Vidyasagar Academy is committed to protecting your privacy. **Eazy PDF Creator** is designed from the ground up as a **100% offline, local-first utility**. We believe your documents, passwords, and private files belong strictly to you.

## 2. No Data Collection or Transmission
- **Zero Telemetry**: Eazy PDF Creator does not collect, record, or transmit any diagnostic, behavioral, or analytical data.
- **Zero Personal Information**: The application does not require account creation, registration, email addresses, or personal details to use.
- **No Network Uploads**: None of your PDF documents, images, text files, annotations, or passwords ever leave your computer. All processing happens entirely within your local machine's memory and disk.

## 3. Local File Processing & Security
- When you open, convert, merge, or protect files, all cryptographic operations (including password protection and permissions locking) are performed locally on your device.
- The application only accesses files and directories that you explicitly choose through open/save dialogs or by dragging and dropping them into the application.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Eazy PDF Creator does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or cloud storage providers.

## 5. Offline Functionality
The application functions completely offline without requiring an active internet connection.
